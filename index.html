Admission Routes & Quotas

Transparent Admission Pathways for 2025–26

Choose the right admission channel tailored to your state domicile, competitive entrance ranking, or merit profile.

Karnataka CET (Government Merit Quota)

For Karnataka domicile candidates who have appeared in KCET conducted by Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA). Seats are allocated through centralized online counseling rounds.

  • ✓ College Code: Use E270 during option entry.
  • ✓ Eligibility: Minimum 45% aggregate in 10+2 Physics & Maths with Chemistry / CS (40% for SC/ST/OBC).
  • ✓ Fee Subsidization: Governed directly by Karnataka Higher Education Department (~₹1.07 Lakh/yr).
  • ✓ Reservation: Full reservation benefits (Rural, Kannada Medium, HK Region 371J).
Enquire for KCET Route
KCET Admission Snapshot Code: E270
Authority: Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)
Counseling Rounds: Round 1, Round 2 & Mop-up Round
Branch Availability: CSE, AIML, ISE, ECE
Annual Govt. Tuition: ₹1,07,000 / year

COMEDK UGET (Pan-India Merit Quota)

Open for students from all states across India. Admission is solely determined by candidate rank in the All India COMEDK Undergraduate Entrance Test.

  • ✓ College Code: Select E175 during COMEDK Web Counseling.
  • ✓ Open Eligibility: 10+2 candidates from CBSE, ICSE, or any recognized state board.
  • ✓ Standardized Fee: Unified fee structure established by COMEDK Karnataka (~₹2.64 Lakh/yr).
  • ✓ Hostel Guarantee: Priority allocation in on-campus student residential quarters.
Enquire for COMEDK Route
COMEDK Snapshot Code: E175
Exam Mode: Computer Based Test (All-India)
Seat Allocation: Online Choice Filling & Counseling
Non-Karnataka Friendly: 100% Eligible without Domicile Barrier
Annual Tuition Fee: ~₹2,64,000 / year

Management / Institutional Merit Quota

Direct institutional admissions handled centrally by the RSST (Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust) Central Office for meritorious applicants seeking early seat confirmation.

  • ✓ Screening: Based on 10+2 PCM scores and entrance test participation (KCET/COMEDK/JEE).
  • ✓ Direct Seat Confirmation: Early provisional booking before general counseling rounds commence.
  • ✓ NRI & Sponsored Seats: Specialized quotas for NRI wards and international applicants.
  • ✓ Personalized Counseling: Dedicated 1-on-1 career guidance with senior faculty.

Lateral Entry for Polytechnic Diploma Holders

Direct admission into the 2nd Year (3rd Semester) of B.E. programs for eligible diploma holders through Karnataka DCET (Diploma Common Entrance Test).

  • ✓ Eligibility: Minimum 45% aggregate in 3-year AICTE-approved Polytechnic Diploma.
  • ✓ Time Saved: Graduate with a full B.E. degree in 3 years.
  • ✓ Bridge Courses: Mathematics and foundational computing bridge modules provided by faculty.
