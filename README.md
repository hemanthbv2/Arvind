# RV Institute of Technology & Management (RVITM) - Admissions Landing Page

A modern, high-converting, and user-friendly admissions landing page built for **RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM), Bengaluru** (Part of the Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust - RSST).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. User-Centric Design & Admissions Pathways
- **Instant Admission Route Clarity:** Dedicated interactive tabs for **KCET (Code: E270)**, **COMEDK UGET (Code: E175)**, **Management / Direct Quota**, and **Diploma Lateral Entry**.
- **Verified Placement Metrics:** Prominently highlights the ₹45.0 LPA highest CTC, 94% placement record, and marquee recruiters (Microsoft, Amazon, Cisco, Bosch, Adobe).
- **Academic Programs Showcase:** In-depth breakdown of 4 industry-aligned B.E. specializations (CSE, AI & ML, ISE, ECE) with intake details and 1-click enquiry triggers.
- **Campus & Innovation Life:** High-resolution campus photography and tech lab showcase highlighting the 100% ICT-enabled smart classrooms.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Instant search filter to quickly answer questions on cutoffs, fees, hostels, and transport.

---

### 2. Low-Friction Admissions Enquiry Form
- **Real-Time Field Validations:**
  - **Full Name:** Validates alphabet characters and minimum length.
  - **Email:** Standard email format checking.
  - **Mobile Number:** 10-digit Indian phone validation (starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9) with automatic non-digit stripping.
  - **Domicile State & Branch Preference:** Clean selection dropdowns.
  - **12th / PUC PCM Percentage:** Live eligibility badge check (>= 45% for general, >= 40% for category).
  - **Consent Checkbox:** Communication authorization.
- **Dynamic Application Reference ID:** Generates a unique tracking code (e.g., `RVITM-2025-XXXXX`) upon submission.
- **Post-Submission Celebration Modal:** Provides an instant brochure download button and direct WhatsApp connectivity to the admissions office.

---

### 3. Integrated AI Admissions Co-Pilot & Chatbot
Combines **Option 1 (Quick-Decision Chips & Admission Q&A)** and **Option 2 (Interactive Conversational Form Co-Pilot)**:
- **Floating Widget & Notification Bubble:** Accessible from any part of the page with a gentle alert bubble.
- **Quick-Prompt Chips:** 1-tap answers for *"Check Eligibility"*, *"Fee Structure & Quotas"*, *"Placement Records 2024"*, and *"College Codes"*.
- **Interactive Form Co-Pilot Mode:**
  - Guides applicants through a friendly conversational interview.
  - **Bi-directional Live Synchronization:** Every step answered in the chat immediately auto-populates and validates the corresponding field on the landing page form!
  - Offers direct submission or review options right from the chat.
- **Synthesized Web Audio Chimes:** Pleasing sound effects when messages arrive or when opening the assistant (with toggle to mute).
